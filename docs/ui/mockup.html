What goes

  • The Explore/Dogfight bar, Flight Controls box, camera button row and quick-control row: all move into one menu (Esc / ☰).
  • Status lines nobody reads: "720 road · 32 rail", "TROPOSPHERE", "SUBSONIC", coordinates, "MODEL CLEARANCE".
  • Coloured pill buttons and the hazard-striped NUKE badge; weapons become small key chips that appear only in combat.

What stays on screen

  • Heading tape, speed and altitude tapes, flight-path marker: thin lines on the world, no boxes.
  • One objective line top-left, a round radar top-right, one toast line, one threat banner.
  • Thrust bar bottom-left, weapons bottom-right, both only as large as they need to be.

Phone

  • Landscape first: a thumb stick on the left, a thrust slider on the right edge, three fire buttons in a thumb arc.
  • Tapes collapse to two numbers beside the marker; radar shrinks to 96 px.
  • Portrait still flies, with a one-time hint to rotate.

Rules for every new screen

  • Colours: symbology, accent, amber, red. Nothing else on the HUD.
  • Type: three sizes (26 / 15 / 11), fixed-width digits, units in small caps after the value.
  • Show a thing only while it changes what the player does.
